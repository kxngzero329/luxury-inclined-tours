# Luxury-Inclined Tours Home Page

## Goal
Build a polished, single-page website for Luxury-Inclined Tours that feels cinematic, discreet, and distinctly Western Cape.

## What I’ll build
- A full-screen photographic opening featuring the supplied headline, supporting copy, and two refined calls to action.
- A slim, elegant navigation bar; every navigation item will return to the top as requested.
- Editorial sections in this order: Signature Experiences, About, Featured Destinations, Why Travel With Us, Luxury Fleet, Testimonials, Bespoke Journey CTA, and Footer.
- Large photographic service and destination stories for Cape Winelands, Cape Point, Garden Route, Safari Experiences, and Executive Travel.
- Responsive layouts for desktop and mobile, with subtle reveals and motion that respect reduced-motion settings.

## Visual direction
- Cormorant Garamond for expressive display headings and Manrope for navigation, body copy, and controls.
- Deep forest green, warm ivory, champagne gold, rich charcoal, stone beige, and soft white as specified.
- Editorial spacing, restrained borders, sharp 4–6px corners, uppercase labels, and no generic rounded SaaS styling.
- Cinematic Western Cape imagery throughout, including a luxury vehicle arriving at a vineyard for the opening.

## Interactions
- “Explore Our Experiences” scrolls to the experience section.
- “Book Your Journey” and the closing CTA open a polished enquiry panel with contact fields and trip preferences; submitting confirms the request locally without adding a database.
- Mobile navigation opens a compact menu; all links return to the top.

## Technical details
- Define all palette, typography, spacing, shadow, and motion choices as reusable design tokens.
- Generate and store a cohesive set of original visual assets for the page.
- Add page-specific search and social metadata.
- Verify the finished page at desktop and mobile sizes, including navigation, scrolling, and enquiry interactions.
